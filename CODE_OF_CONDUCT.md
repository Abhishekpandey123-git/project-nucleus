# Code of Conduct

## Project Nucleus Community Standards

### Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in Project Nucleus a respectful, inclusive, collaborative, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

Project Nucleus is an open-source research initiative focused on neural networks, multimodal artificial intelligence, and scalable learning systems.

We welcome contributors regardless of their experience level, educational background, nationality, gender, identity, or technical expertise.

We believe that great research and engineering emerge from curiosity, collaboration, and mutual respect.

### Our Standards

All community members are expected to demonstrate positive behavior, including:

- Being respectful and considerate toward others.
- Welcoming newcomers and helping them understand the project.
- Providing constructive technical feedback.
- Respecting different opinions, approaches, and research perspectives.
- Accepting constructive criticism gracefully.
- Giving appropriate credit to original authors and contributors.
- Communicating honestly about technical results and limitations.
- Maintaining a collaborative and professional environment.

### Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Offensive, insulting, or derogatory comments.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or sustained hostile behavior.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Plagiarism or misrepresenting another person's work.
- Deliberately falsifying research results or benchmarks.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or inappropriate conduct.
- Any behavior that creates an unsafe or hostile community environment.

### Research and Technical Integrity

Because Project Nucleus is a research-oriented initiative, contributors must also:

- Report experiments and benchmarks honestly.
- Clearly distinguish original implementations from reused work.
- Respect software, dataset, and model licenses.
- Avoid fabricating experimental results.
- Acknowledge contributions and intellectual work appropriately.
- Respect confidentiality and data privacy.

Technical disagreements are encouraged when they remain respectful and evidence-based.

### Maintainer Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for:

- Clarifying and enforcing community standards.
- Reviewing reported incidents fairly.
- Taking appropriate and proportionate corrective action.
- Protecting the privacy of individuals involved in reports.
- Providing a clear communication channel for concerns.
- Applying these standards consistently.

Maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, temporarily restrict participation, or remove community access when necessary.

### Reporting Violations

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it to the project maintainers.

**GitHub repository:** https://github.com/Abhishekpandey123-git/project-nucleus

You may contact the maintainers through GitHub or open a private communication channel where available.

When reporting an incident, include:

- A description of what happened.
- Relevant links, messages, or evidence.
- The date and context of the incident.
- Any additional information that may help the maintainers investigate.

Reports will be handled with discretion and reviewed as promptly as reasonably possible.

### Enforcement Guidelines

Depending on the severity and circumstances, maintainers may take the following actions:

1. **Correction:** A private or public request to correct inappropriate behavior.
2. **Warning:** A formal warning explaining the violation.
3. **Temporary restriction:** Temporary suspension of participation in project spaces.
4. **Permanent restriction:** Removal from project participation for serious or repeated violations.

Maintainers will consider the context, impact, severity, and history of the behavior when determining an appropriate response.

### Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all Project Nucleus community spaces, including:

- GitHub repositories and issues.
- Pull requests and code reviews.
- Discussions and project communication channels.
- Project-related online events and meetings.
- Other spaces where an individual represents Project Nucleus.
